# Focus rules

## Focus rules

- **What it is.** A Focus space is a work timer in the Pomodoro style: focus, short break, and a long break after every few sessions (25, 5, 15 and 4 by default). Each finished focus phase is saved as a session in `focus_sessions` with what it was about. The space's lengths are one `focus_settings` row (none means the defaults in `focus-schemas.ts`).
- **Tasks.** Sessions can carry a task (`taskId`, `taskTitle`, copied in), but the page has no way to pick one for now; the task picker was taken out at the owner's request. Sessions are saved with no task and count as "No task" in the stats.
- **One timer per browser** (`FocusTimerProviderComponent` in `AppShellComponent`). It is kept in `localStorage` (`daybook-focus-timer`) as an end time while running, or the time left while paused. So it keeps counting while you work elsewhere in Daybook, survives a reload, and follows you across tabs (the `storage` event). The provider reads storage before anything may replace it.
  - The timer belongs to one Focus space. Another Focus space's page says where it is running and won't start until it is finished or reset.
  - `FocusPillComponent` floats on every page but `/focus` once a Focus space has been opened by someone who can edit it, idle or not. It starts above the Assistant button and can be dragged anywhere by its body. A press becomes a drag after 4 px, so clicks still work, and the click after a drag is swallowed. With the pill focused, the arrow keys move it. It is kept inside the window, and its spot is remembered per browser (`daybook-focus-pill-position`). It shows the time left and the phase, starts, pauses or resumes, and links back. An idle timer can be hidden (`clear`), which forgets it until a Focus space is opened again.
  - The rules live in `focus-math.ts`. Ending a focus phase records a session and waits on a short break (a long one after every `longBreakEvery`th). Ending a break waits on focus. Nothing starts by itself. Skip moves on without recording or counting. Reset drops a session under way. The phase can only be switched while nothing is under way. New lengths apply at once to an idle timer, and from the next phase to one under way.
- **When a phase ends** a tone plays (`shared/utils/chime.ts`, shared with the Fitness rest timer) and a toast shows. A desktop notification also shows if the tab is in the background and permission was given; it is asked for on the first Start.
- **Saving a session.** The browser sends `POST /focus/:spaceId/sessions` with its own id, and the server ignores a repeat (`onConflictDoNothing`), so a retry or two tabs finishing the same session record it once. There is no retry queue: a session lost to a dropped connection is gone.
- **Routes** (`/focus`, the space must be a Focus space or it answers 404): `GET /:spaceId?from&to` (view; the unlock cookie is needed) returns `{ settings, sessions }`. `PUT /:spaceId/settings` and `POST /:spaceId/sessions` need edit access and write under the space owner. The web loads the last 7 days (`['focus', spaceId, from, to]`).
- **Stats** are computed in the browser from those sessions: sessions and focus time today, minutes per day for 7 days, and the week's time per task (`byTask`, sessions without a task count together as "No task").
- **Space rules.** Can be shared: viewers see the stats, not the timer. Can't be duplicated. Rows go when the space is deleted for good.
