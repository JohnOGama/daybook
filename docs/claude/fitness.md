# Fitness rules

## Fitness rules

- Whole numbers, no floats: food weight and macros in milligrams (`...Mg`), energy in whole kcal, body weight in grams (`weightG`), height in millimetres, a serving count in thousandths (`quantityMilli`). Food nutrition is stored per 100 g.
- Built-in foods (USDA SR Legacy) and exercises (free-exercise-db) are shared rows with no `spaceId` or `userId`, loaded by `fitness:import-foods` and `fitness:import-exercises`; they are read-only through the API. Custom foods, servings and exercises carry the space and owner. A serving can be added to a built-in food and belongs to the space that added it.
- A logged food (`fitness_food_logs`) stores its own name, amount and numbers, so editing or deleting a food never changes a past day. Workouts and templates store each exercise's name and muscles the same way.
- Meal plans, workout templates and workouts each keep their items as one JSON document, checked by zod on the way in and replaced whole on save.
- Nothing is logged automatically and nothing recurs. Applying a meal plan or starting a workout from a template copies it onto the day as planned rows (`eaten: false`, `done: false`); planned rows never count towards totals, averages, muscle load or the forecast until they are ticked.
- Targets, BMI, day totals, the forecast and muscle load are computed in the browser (`targets-math.ts`, `food-math.ts`, `forecast-math.ts`, `workout-math.ts`); the API only stores and checks ownership. Days with nothing eaten are left out of averages, never counted as zero.
- The body diagram's shapes (`body-shapes.ts`) are from react-body-highlighter (MIT, notice in the file). Its one upper-back region stands for both lats and middle back.
- The AI read reuses `BudgetAiService.fitnessRead` through `POST /fitness/ai/read`. It is sent only what `fitness-summary.ts` builds: figures, never food, exercise or workout names.
- AI apps log food, workouts and weigh-ins through MCP (`docs/claude/mcp.md`) under the same rules: one-off foods are logs only, never stored foods.
- Recent foods (`GET /fitness/foods/recent`) carry `last`, the amount and serving logged last time, so the Add food list can log them again in one tap (`lastItem` in `food-math.ts`) and opens them at that amount.
- **Onboarding** (`FitnessSetupComponent`, `?view=setup`, no tab of its own). It replaces the whole page, without the title and tabs, centred in the full height so no step scrolls. Today opens straight into it for an editor whose profile isn't set up (`hasProfile` in `targets-math.ts`: sex, birth date and height, or manual macros); "Skip for now" lasts for the visit, so it opens again next time until the profile is set up; `?view=setup` always opens it. Viewers never see it. Four steps: sex, birth date, height and activity; today's weight and an optional goal weight (with BMI); goal and pace (lose 0.25–1, gain 0.25–0.5 kg a week, with the goal date and the daily deficit or surplus); then the daily targets. Nothing is saved until "Save and start", which writes the same profile Settings edits and today's weight as a weigh-in (skipped when today already has that weight).
- **AI macros** (`POST /fitness/ai/macros`, `BudgetAiService.suggestMacros`, edit access) are asked for on entering the last step. Only figures are sent, checked by `fitnessMacrosSchema`: age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, goal, pace, activity and the daily calorie target. The reply is protein in g per kg and the carbs share of the rest; anything outside 1.2–2.4 g/kg or 20–60 % is refused (502), never clamped. It is saved as the `perKg` macro method. Without `GEMINI_API_KEY`, or when the AI fails, the standard `balanced` preset is used and labelled so.
- Settings asks before a link or tab close throws away unsaved changes.
- Water is a glass count per day (`fitness_water`, one row per space and day, 0 to 30, `PUT /fitness/water`), returned with the day range as `water`. The goal is a fixed 8 glasses (`WATER_GOAL`).
- An empty meal on Today offers "Same as yesterday", which copies yesterday's eaten rows for that meal as new eaten rows, with Undo. It only happens when tapped.
- Today (`FitnessTodayComponent`) is a week strip (`WeekStripComponent`: the 7 days up to the shown one, each with a calorie bar; it is the day picker), a summary card (`MacroMetersComponent`: calories left, macros, and water, sets and weight at a glance), one Meals list (`MealsCardComponent`), the Workout card (`TodayWorkoutCardComponent`) holding the large muscle map with its day/7-day toggle, and Water and Weight beside them. On extra-large screens (`xl`) Today fits the screen with no page scroll: the page, both `<Skeleton>`s and the day grid pass the height down, the page drops its `max-w-6xl` cap so it uses the full width, and the Workout card runs the full height on the far right beside the summary card and Meals | Water and Weight (from `lg` it is always the right-hand column) that each scroll inside themselves, and the muscle map is sized by the card's height. Below `xl` the page scrolls as normal. The Workout card has no per-set ticking: **Complete workout** marks every set of that workout done in one `saveWorkout`, with Undo in the toast (the same `from` object carries the new version to the Undo save). Set-by-set ticking and the rest timer live on the Workouts tab. The Weight card (`WeightCardComponent`) draws the last 30 days of weigh-ins as a rounded line (midpoint curves, so it never overshoots a real value) on a half-kilo kg scale with three labels, with a dot per weigh-in, and always shows a weigh-in field for the shown day (not future days); a second weigh-in on the same day replaces it.
- The Calendar tab (`FitnessCalendarComponent`, `?view=calendar`) shows a month (the `day` param's month, Monday first, through `shared/utils/month-grid.ts`) from one `useFitnessDays` range: each day's eaten calories (in `destructive` over the target), its workouts (filled when every set is done) and its weigh-in. Clicking a day opens it on Today; the month arrows are links, so the month is in the URL. It is read only.
- Ticking a set starts a 90 s rest timer (`RestTimerComponent`, +30 s or Skip, a tone at the end). Under "Last time" each exercise suggests a small step up (`nextTry`: +2.5 kg at the same reps, or one more rep; nothing for timed exercises).
- There is no offline queue and a Fitness space cannot be duplicated.
