# Job hunt rules

## Job hunt rules

- A job's status is `saved`, `applied`, `interviewing`, `offer` or `rejected` (shown as "Not accepted"). The current status is a column; every change also appends `{ kind: 'status', status, day }` to the job's `events` JSON, and a follow-up appends `{ kind: 'followUp', day }`. "Applied" is the earliest `applied` day, always read from days, never from array order.
- Waiting days, the "no reply" nudge (7 days; "close it?" from 30), the tiles, tab counts, search and sort are computed in the browser by `features/jobs/src/jobs-math.ts`. Nothing ever changes a status by itself.
- Adding from a link: the browser calls `POST /jobs/ai/read-posting`, and only creates the job once the details come back; a login wall, an unreadable page or a non-posting opens the manual form with the reason instead. Pasting a link already in the list (compared with `normalizeLink`) selects that job.
- The server fetches posting pages itself in `posting-page.ts`: http(s) only, no cookies, at most 3 redirects, 10 s, 2 MB, and every connection's resolved address must pass `isPublicAddress` (checked in the socket `lookup`, so redirects and DNS rebinding can't reach private addresses).
- The AI goes through `BudgetAiService` (`readPosting`, `matchJob`). `readPosting` is sent only that page's text and JobPosting JSON-LD; `matchJob` only the CV and one job's role, company and description. Notes, statuses and other jobs are never sent. The match runs when a job is added (if there is a CV and a description) and on Recheck; editing the CV marks matches stale instead of re-running them.
- The CV is plain text in `job_profiles`, one per space. Jobs are hard-deleted with Undo (re-created with the same id, events and match). A Job hunt space cannot be duplicated and has no offline queue.
- The interview date is the job's next step (`nextStepDay`, `nextStepNote`), asked for on moving to Interviewing and editable in the Status card while there. It only counts while the job is in Interviewing (`bookedStep`): one kept after moving on to Offer or Not accepted stays stored but never shows as upcoming in the table, the tiles or the calendar.
- The page switches between the table and a month calendar (`JobsCalendarComponent`, `calendarEntries`). The calendar shows booked interviews highlighted, plus every status change and follow-up from `events`, for the jobs the current tab and search show. Clicking an entry opens that job. The month helpers are shared with List in `shared/utils/month-grid.ts`.
- **Interview reminders.** A next step can have a time. The browser turns the day and time into one moment in the person's own time zone (`stepInstant`) and saves it as `nextStepAt`; with no time there is no moment and no email. `InterviewReminderScheduler` (`interview-reminders.ts`, every minute, not under vitest) emails the space owner through Resend (`createInterviewReminderSender`, the same `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` as sign-in) once a job in Interviewing is 30 minutes or less from `nextStepAt`. It skips spaces that aren't Job hunt or are in Trash (including through a folder or group, via `viewerIds`). `reminderSentFor` records which `nextStepAt` was reminded: it is claimed before sending, so ticks can't double-send, and released if sending fails, so the next tick retries. A moved time is reminded again. A time already passed is never reminded late, and nothing is sent while the API is down. Only the owner gets the email, never people the space is shared with. The email holds the role, company, note and a link to the space (`BETTER_AUTH_URL`), never a clock time, since the server doesn't know the time zone.
- A job update changes only the fields sent: `updateJobSchema` drops the defaults of `jobFields`, so a status change never blanks the description, notes or interview time.
