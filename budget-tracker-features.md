# Personal Budget Tracker

## Description

A personal finance application that helps users manage their money in
one place. Users can track income and expenses, monitor account
balances, create budgets, manage recurring salary and bills, set savings
goals, and understand their spending habits.

The goal is to make everyday money management simple while providing
useful tools for planning future finances.

## Features

### 1. Dashboard

-   View total balance across accounts.
-   See total income, expenses, and remaining budget.
-   View recent transactions.
-   See upcoming bills and scheduled income.
-   View monthly spending summaries.

### 2. Income and Expense Tracking

-   Add, edit, and delete income and expenses.
-   Assign categories such as food, transportation, utilities, salary,
    and shopping.
-   Add notes, dates, and payment accounts.
-   Search and filter transaction history.
-   Attach receipt images.

### 3. Account Management

-   Manage cash, bank accounts, savings accounts, and e-wallets.
-   View individual account balances.
-   Transfer money between accounts.
-   Track credit card balances.

### 4. Categories

-   Use built-in income and expense categories.
-   Create custom categories.
-   Organize spending by category.
-   View category-based spending summaries.

### 5. Budget Management

-   Set monthly or weekly spending limits.
-   Create budgets for individual categories.
-   Track actual spending against the budget.
-   See how much budget remains.
-   Receive alerts when approaching or exceeding a budget.
-   Carry unused budget into the next period.

### 6. Recurring Income and Expenses

-   Schedule recurring salary, allowances, rent, bills, and
    subscriptions.
-   Support daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly schedules.
-   Set specific payment dates, such as the 15th and last day of each
    month.
-   Set start and end dates.
-   Automatically record scheduled transactions or require user
    confirmation.
-   Pause, resume, skip, or cancel recurring schedules.
-   Receive reminders for upcoming transactions.

### 7. Bills and Subscriptions

-   Track monthly bills and subscriptions.
-   Set payment due dates and amounts.
-   Mark bills as paid or unpaid.
-   Receive upcoming and overdue payment reminders.
-   View total monthly subscription costs.

### 8. Savings Goals

-   Create savings goals with target amounts.
-   Set target dates.
-   Record contributions.
-   Track progress toward each goal.
-   View the remaining amount needed.

### 9. Debt Management

-   Track loans and credit card balances.
-   Record payments.
-   Monitor remaining debt.
-   Track payment due dates and interest rates.
-   View debt payment history.

### 10. Reports and Analytics

-   View income and expense reports.
-   See spending breakdowns by category.
-   Compare monthly spending.
-   Track savings rates.
-   Identify highest spending categories.
-   Export transaction reports.

### 11. Cash Flow Forecasting

-   Estimate future account balances.
-   Include scheduled salary and recurring expenses.
-   View upcoming financial commitments.
-   Receive warnings about projected low balances.
-   Try hypothetical purchases to see how they affect future balances.

### 12. Additional Features

-   Multiple currency support.
-   CSV import and export.
-   Receipt scanning.
-   Automatic expense categorization.
-   AI-generated spending summaries.
-   Offline transaction recording.
-   Data backup and export.

## Suggested Main Navigation

-   **Home** -- Financial overview and upcoming activity.
-   **Transactions** -- Income, expenses, and transfers.
-   **Budgets** -- Spending limits and budget progress.
-   **Reports** -- Spending trends and financial summaries.
-   **Settings** -- Accounts, categories, preferences, and security.

## Important Rules

-   Transfers between a user's own accounts should not count as income
    or expenses.
-   Scheduled income should not affect the actual balance until it is
    received or confirmed.
-   Recurring transactions should not be recorded more than once.
-   Savings transfers should not be counted as spending.
-   Reports should distinguish actual transactions from future
    estimates.
